  <p class="dl-lede">WAV Keep takes a public song page and saves the audio and video files that page already publishes, to your device.

<section class="dl-sec key">
  <h2>1. No warranties</h2>
  <p>The tool is provided <b>as it is</b>, without warranty of any kind — not for uninterrupted availability, not for a particular
  output, not for fitness for any purpose. In plain terms:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>A link that works today may resolve to nothing tomorrow. The file behind a public song page is the platform's file, on the
        platform's infrastructure, and <b>we do not control either</b>.</li>
    <li>Saved output is <b>the same bytes the platform published</b>. If a file is silent, short, missing a video track, or differs
        from what you expected, that came from the source page — not from a degradation we introduced.</li>
    <li>We keep no copy. If you close the tab before the file lands, it is gone; check your browser's downloads folder, then re-run.</li>
    <li>You use this at your own risk, including the risk that <b>a plan you hold changes what you may download</b>, or that a
        platform disables downloads altogether.</li>
  </ul>
</section>

<section class="dl-sec warn">
  <h2>2. What we don't control: the platforms change the rules</h2>
  <p>This is the realistic failure mode for a tool like ours, so we're naming it instead of hiding it behind general words.</p>
  <ul>
    <li><b>Suno</b> has already rewritten its download policy once (announced in its help center, effective September 2026), setting
        per-plan download limits. Whatever limits apply to your account apply whether or not you use us.</li>
    <li><b>Udio</b> switched off downloading of audio, video and stems entirely as part of its Universal Music Group announcement —
        its own help center states it in those words. We do not and cannot restore that; see
        <a href="/udio-downloader/">the Udio page</a> for what we say and why.</li>
    <li>Announcements like these are <b>edited and re-dated by the platform</b>, and Udio's public changelog has logged no entry since
        May 2025. When something changes, we update our pages to match — we don't get to decide it, and we don't argue with it.</li>
  </ul>
  <div class="dl-quote">Everything we attribute to Suno or Udio on this site is quoted from their own help pages, with a link and a
  date. Where we have not verified something first-hand, we say so rather than implying it works.</div>
</section>

<section class="dl-sec">
  <h2>3. What we won't do</h2>
  <ol>
    <li><b>No watermark removal.</b> Suno has announced watermarking and fingerprinting embedded in the audio itself, not as an
        overlay on screen. Any "removal" means re-encoding your track. We save files as published and don't alter them, so
        <b>we don't claim a clean output, and no tool honestly can</b>. See
        <a href="/suno-watermark/">the watermark page</a>.</li>
    <li><b>No access to what isn't published.</b> Unreleased, private or unlisted songs have no public page for us to read, and we
        won't attempt to reach them. If you send us a link with a token or share secret in it, we won't use it.</li>
    <li><b>No getting you a paid feature for free.</b> If a format or a function sits behind a plan, we'll tell you it sits behind
        a plan and link to the official page that says so. We won't help you get around it.</li>
    <li><b>No browser extensions, no scripts running on Suno's pages.</b> Anything that behaves that way is out of scope for us —
        the <a href="/suno-bulk-download/">bulk export page</a> explains why we draw the line where we do.</li>
  </ol>
</section>

<section class="dl-sec">
  <h2>4. Your files, and whose permission you need</h2>
  <p>Whatever rights you hold in a song you generated stay yours and are governed by <b>the platform's own terms and plan</b>, not by
  anything on this site. Two questions we get asked and deliberately won't answer:</p>
  <ul>
    <li><b>"Can I release this on Spotify / Apple Music / YouTube?"</b> — That's Suno's licensing terms plus your distributor's policy.
        We have no visibility into your plan or your generation history and won't guess.</li>
    <li><b>"Can I use this commercially?"</b> — Same answer: the platform's terms decide, not us.</li>
  </ul>
  <p>If you don't own or control the content you paste, <b>stop</b> — saving someone else's song doesn't give you a licence to it, and
  that's on you, not on this tool.</p>
</section>

<section class="dl-sec">
  <h2>5. What we don't claim about audio quality</h2>

  or better than what you get from the platform's own button, because we have no basis for it.</p>
