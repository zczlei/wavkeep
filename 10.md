<div class="hero">
  <h1>About WAV Keep</h1>
  <p class="ab-lede">A small web tool that takes your own songs out of Suno — keeping the file that Suno's public song page
  already serves, rather than re-rendering or re-encoding it. This page explains how it works, who's behind it,
  and the four things we deliberately refuse to promise.</p>
</div>

<section>
  <h2 class="ab-h">Why this exists</h2>
  <p>Two platform changes in late 2025 and 2026 pushed people into the same spot: they had songs they had made, and no easy way
  to get the files onto their own machine. Suno tightened its download rules in <b>September 2026</b>; Udio switched downloads off
  entirely in <b>October 2025</b> as part of its Universal Music Group agreement. Between them, "how do I keep my own song" went from
  a non-question to a search box.</p>
  <p>WAV Keep answers the half of that question that has an honest answer. Where a public Suno page already publishes a file,
  we let you take a copy of it — WAV, MP3, cover art, and where the song has one, the video — without going through the
  download button on suno.com, so the copy costs you nothing from your allowance. Where a file was never published,
  we say so and stop.</p>
</section>

<section>
  <h2 class="ab-h">What it does, in plain words</h2>
  <ol>
    <li>You paste a <b>public</b> link — a song page, a playlist, or a profile.</li>
    <li>We read that page and list what it actually publishes.</li>
    <li>You pick the formats you want; we hand you each file as it is served.</li>
  </ol>
  <p>Three things follow from that, and they're the reason some requests fail: a song you never shared publicly has no public page,
  so there is nothing to read; a song without a video has no video file, and we won't render one to make the count look better;
  and a file is what it is — we don't re-encode it and then describe the result as something it isn't.</p>
  <p>No sign-in, no account, no app to install. It runs from this page and asks for no permission on any other site — which is why
  it isn't a browser extension.</p>
</section>

<section>
  <h2 class="ab-h">What we won't promise</h2>
  <table class="ab">
    <thead><tr><th>Claim</th><th>Our answer</th><th>Why</th></tr></thead>
    <tbody>
      <tr><td>“Watermark remover / watermark-free audio”</td>
          <td class="n">We don't do this</td>
          <td>Suno announced audio watermarking and fingerprinting for generated songs, with reports describing the mark as carried
              <em>inside</em> the audio. Taking it out means processing the whole track. We hand back the published file as published —
              mark included — and point you to <a href="/suno-watermark/">what the mark is</a> instead of claiming around it.</td></tr>
      <tr><td>“Stem splitter / download stems from a link”</td>
          <td class="n">We don't do this</td>
          <td>Stems aren't separate files sitting on a public song page; a public page publishes the finished mix. Suno's own help
              pages describe exporting individual tracks through <b>Studio</b>, which is on the Premier plan. Details on
              <a href="/suno-stems/">the stems page</a>.</td></tr>
      <tr><td>“Udio downloader”</td>
          <td class="n">Nobody can, right now</td>
          <td>Udio's help center states that downloading of audio, video, and stems has been disabled. We keep a page explaining
              <a href="/udio-downloader/">what was switched off and when</a>, and we don't sell a workaround for it.</td></tr>
      <tr><td>“Lossless / uncompressed / studio master quality”</td>
          <td class="n">We don't write this</td>
          <td>Our WAV export carries the audio from the file the page serves, at 48 kHz, ready for a DAW. What it does is
              <b>no second lossy pass</b> on our side. Calling the result a master would be marketing, not a description.</td></tr>
      <tr><td>“We're the official Suno downloader”</td>
          <td class="n">Absolutely not</td>
          <td>WAV Keep is independent. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Suno or Udio —
              see <a href="/terms/">our Terms</a>.</td></tr>
    </tbody>
  </table>
  <p class="ab-note"><b>If a page on this site ever sounds like a promise we've refused here, that's a bug in our copy, not a feature.</b>
  Forward it to the address below and we'll fix the wording.</p>
</section>
