  the fastest fix for most issues is the <a href="/suno-watermark/">watermark page</a> or the FAQ on the page you were using:
  the questions we get most often are already answered there.</p>

<section>
  <h2 class="ct-h">Include this in your first message</h2>
  <div class="ct-list">
    <ol>
      <li><b>The exact link you pasted</b> — the song, playlist or profile URL. This is the single thing that lets us reproduce
          a failure. <code>https://suno.com/song/…</code></li>
      <li><b>What you asked for</b> — which formats you selected (WAV / MP3 / cover art / video).</li>
      <li><b>What came back</b> — the error text as written, or "the file opened but was silent", or "nothing downloaded".
          A screenshot helps if the words are awkward.</li>
      <li><b>Where you are</b> — browser and rough device (Chrome on Windows, Safari on iPhone), and the approximate time.</li>
      <li><b>Whether the song is public</b> — a private or unlisted song has no public page to read, and that's usually the whole
          answer. You don't need to send us a password or a session token; we never ask for either.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
</section>

<section>
  <h2 class="ct-h">How quickly you'll hear back</h2>
  <div class="ct-reply">
    <h3>A few business days, not a 24-hour service</h3>
    <p>WAV Keep is maintained by one person alongside everything else. We read <b>support@</b> weekly and usually reply within a few
    business days; <b>dmca@</b> and <b>abuse@</b> are checked more often and actioned first when a notice is complete.
    If you haven't heard back in two weeks, resend — mail does get lost, and a second message isn't an imposition.</p>
  </div>
  <p>No ticketing system, no phone line, no live chat. Everything runs through email so there's a written trail — which matters most
  exactly when it matters: copyright notices.</p>
</section>

<section>
  <h2 class="ct-h">One more thing before you write</h2>
  <p class="ct-lede"><b>Never send</b> your Suno password, an account session, a private share link with a token, or anything else that
  lets someone act as you. We have no use for it, we won't ask for it, and if a message arrives containing it we delete the message
  rather than read the file. Everything this tool needs is already in a public link.</p>
